## Description

This exceptional six-bedroom villa, awarded by the European Property Awards in the category of Single Residence for Portugal, is the creation of  architect Maider Neto and redefines modern luxury in Quinta do Lago. A masterpiece of design, the residence blends sophistication, comfort, and practicality in a way that is unparalleled.Set in the highly sought-after and secure resort of Quinta do Lago, the villa enjoys commanding views over the golf course, lake, and sea, and lies within walking distance of the celebrated Ria Formosa Nature Reserve. Its south-facing orientation ensures abundant natural light and uninterrupted panoramic views throughout the day, heightening the sense of serene and luxurious living.The entrance makes a striking first impression, framed by a living wall and tranquil water feature leading to a grand main door. Inside, the carved staircase and lift connect all four levels of the residence. The open-plan living area is a showcase of modern elegance, with double-height ceilings and a statement staircase that separates the grand hall from the refined sitting room.The villa offers six bespoke bedroom suites, each with private en-suite bathrooms. The master suite stands out for its exclusivity, featuring two luxurious bathrooms, a private terrace with sweeping views, a dressing area, and a dedicated laundry room for convenience.At the heart of the home lies the spacious Loxley kitchen, fitted with premium appliances including a Bora induction hob and Siemens and Liebherr products. Adjoining the kitchen, the dining area and cosy sitting room with feature fireplace create the perfect setting for family living and elegant entertaining.The rooftop terrace is a showpiece in itself, offering breathtaking views of the ocean, lake, and golf course. A heated 17sqm pool makes it an ideal retreat for relaxation or social gatherings against the spectacular backdrop.The basement level enhances the villa’s lifestyle credentials, with a private office, state-of-the-art cinema room, gym, large sauna, and space for a golf simulator. The layout also allows for the creation of a self-contained maid’s apartment, offering complete independence and privacy.Every detail has been meticulously considered. The villa is equipped with advanced home technology including a fully integrated sound system, motorised curtains, underfloor heating, and centralised automation. Eco-conscious features, such as a borehole and solar panels for water heating, further enrich the property’s appeal.Positioned in one of the Algarve’s most prestigious enclaves, Quinta do Lago offers world-class golf, pristine beaches, fine dining, and an array of leisure opportunities.

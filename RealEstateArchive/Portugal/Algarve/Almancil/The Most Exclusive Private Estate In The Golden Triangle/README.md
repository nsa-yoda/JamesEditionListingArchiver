Ultimate Privacy
The estate offers a level of privacy that is truly rare within the Golden Triangle. While most properties — even those priced between €10M and €20M — are set on relatively small plots, Quinta do Tesoura stands apart.
With three separate entrances and 5 hectares of land, you enjoy complete freedom and discretion, while still being close to beaches, restaurants and all amenities.

The Golden Triangle
The Golden Triangle — including Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo and Almancil — is widely regarded as the most prestigious area in the Algarve. Known for its luxury lifestyle, world-class golf, fine dining and international community, it continues to attract high-end buyers from across Europe and beyond.
 
Investment &amp; Development Potential
The Golden Triangle remains one of Europe’s strongest real estate markets.
 
For visionary buyers, this estate may offer interesting future possibilities, subject to applicable regulations and approvals. Its unique position outside the agricultural reserve creates potential for a boutique countryside concept or private retreat, integrated into the natural surroundings.

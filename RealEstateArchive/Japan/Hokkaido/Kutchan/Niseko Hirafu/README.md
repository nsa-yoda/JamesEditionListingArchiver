## Description

H2 Christie's International Real Estate proudly presents&nbsp;Hakuchōzan, a seven-bedroom luxury ski chalet in the prestigious Hirafu Village of Niseko. Japanese for 'Swan Mountain,' Hakuchōzan was inspired by Neuschwanstein Castle in Bavaria, with the swan symbolizing grace, beauty, and enduring love. Conceived as a multi-generational family retreat, the chalet blends communal spaces and private nooks, allowing loved ones to gather or enjoy quiet reflection, offering multiple communal spaces, private nooks, and panoramic views of Mt.

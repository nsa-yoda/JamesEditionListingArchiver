Hand-selected Italian marble. Warm brass accents. Rare natural timbers. A sophisticated lighting system that choreographs the sensory experience of the space from morning to night. The result is not an interior. It is livable art — and it required, in its commission, between 18 and 24 months of lead time that this residence has already absorbed.

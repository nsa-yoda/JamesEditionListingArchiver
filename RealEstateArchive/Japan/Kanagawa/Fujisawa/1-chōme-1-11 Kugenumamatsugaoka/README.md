## Description

① Property Highlights
The estate was completed in 2019 across 801.59 sqm (8,628 sq ft) of freehold coastal land. Reinforced concrete, two floors and basement, cedar and glass softening the structure's presence. Elevator across all levels. Full air-conditioning and underfloor heating throughout. A garden-level spa opens toward camellia gardens and greenery. Upper levels frame shifting light — morning over the ocean, evening over Fuji.

The configuration — a three-bedroom wing alongside a fully self-contained two-bedroom suite — offers operational flexibility that few estates of this nature provide: primary residence with independent guest accommodation, multi-generational family living, or private staff quarters, without compromise in any direction.

The horizon from the rooftop terrace — Sagami Bay to the south, Enoshima at the edge of the water, Mount Fuji rising with quiet clarity to the northwest — is not a view that today happens to exist. It is a view the law has guaranteed will not be taken.

Coastal freehold land of this scale, within a protected low-rise zone, no longer comes to market in this part of Japan. That sentence will only become more true with time.

To own this estate is not to acquire a residence. It is to secure a legacy — measured not in years, but in generations.

② The Neighbourhood
There are coastlines that are beautiful. And there are coastlines that are protected — legally, permanently, and in a way that means what you see today is precisely what will remain. The Shōnan coast is Japan's most celebrated coastal lifestyle destination — the stretch of Pacific shoreline southwest of Tokyo that has long drawn those seeking space, nature, and a quieter cadence of living. It is to Tokyo what the Hamptons are to New York, or the Côte d'Azur to Paris.

And within it, Kugenuma Matsugaoka sits in a first-class low-rise residential and scenic preservation district where vertical development and subdivision are legally prohibited.

Kugenuma Station is a three-minute walk. Central Tokyo is approximately 60 minutes by direct rail. Kamakura's historic streets and temples are minutes away. Enoshima's waterfront is within immediate reach.

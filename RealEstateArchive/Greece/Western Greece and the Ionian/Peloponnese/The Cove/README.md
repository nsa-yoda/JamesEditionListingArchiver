## Description

In a prime, rare to find location, your private paradise awaits within this exclusive seafront estate that comprises two stunning villas with pools that are set within beautiful gardens and enjoy unrivalled panoramic sea, coast and mainland views from every spot. Add in the private access to a stunning cove and beach with its private jetty and you have the perfect property that promises an unparalleled lifestyle.

Situated in Barbati, one of the most sought-after locations on the northeast coast, just 16 km from Corfu Town and the airport, and only a 10-minute boat ride away, this fabulous estate is set within a sprawling 5,400 sqm seafront plot and offers the perfect blend of luxury and privacy whilst having easy access to all amenities and facilities.
There is private access via a concreted road to one of the most beautiful, secluded beaches on the northeast coast that comes complete with storage area, a fully equipped outdoor kitchen, a BBQ area, a pizza oven, and ample dining space for up to 20 guests—all set against the soothing backdrop of the waves. Whether you access the beach by golf cart, car, or on foot, you can indulge in the serene beauty of this coastal paradise.

From every corner of the property, you can enjoy the panoramic views that stretch across the sea and coast towards Corfu Town and its iconic fortress, as well as having views of the mainland. The southerly facing gardens are truly unique with an elevated position on the rock offering a serene landscape and tranquil ambiance.

Villa 1 is a modern and luxurious smart home with a bespoke quality and design that creates an ambiance that is both minimalistic and homely, while maximizing the magnificent views. It offers 233 sqm of living space over two levels with wonderful sea-facing covered terraces and balconies. The spacious villa houses a living room and dining area, both with access out to the terraces. There is a fully fitted, well-equipped kitchen, three ensuite bedrooms, a versatile office space, two WCs, a luxurious bathroom, and a sauna.

A lovely pathway through the magical landscaped gardens leads to the infinity pool and the wonderful external covered seating and dining area – all perfectly positioned to offer stunning sea views – the perfect spot to unwind or entertain. Adjacent to the pool, there is a convenient storage and sauna area of 16 sqm. Just beyond the pool, hidden among the trees, is a BBQ area/

Villa 2 is a contemporary classic-style home that harmoniously blends luxury and modern design with the enchanting Greek and Venetian architecture. It offers an impressive 372 sqm of living space spread across three levels, plus a spacious basement. The ground floor features a fully fitted and equipped kitchen, sitting room, dining area and guest WC. There is access from this level out to a large terrace and a wonderful infinity pool.

Ascend to the first floor and you'll find an oversized master suite complete with a private sitting area, an expansive terrace, and a state-of-the-art bathroom featuring both a luxurious jacuzzi and a separate shower bath - nearly 60 sqm of pure indulgence. The attic space offers two bedrooms, each with its own ensuite bathroom. Finally, the basement features storage and laundry spaces as well as a large office area that could be converted into a gymnasium or cinema.

## Description

Beyond the ordinary and far from the expected, a world of elevated Mediterranean living unfolds, where refined design, absolute privacy, and breathtaking beauty converge in perfect harmony.

Perched above the crystalline waters of the Ionian Sea, this amazing villa is an exquisite, multi-level waterfront estate that commands panoramic views across the Greek coastline. Nestled into the golden cliffs of Corfu, this exceptional villa harmoniously integrates with its dramatic natural surroundings, offering a secluded and elegant escape mere steps from the sea.

Inspired by the architecture of a medieval fortress, the villa exudes timeless sophistication while embracing contemporary comfort.
The villa is set within a private estate of 8,012 square meters offering a total interior space of 960 square meters. Designed to flow effortlessly across multiple levels, the villa’s open-plan layout is flooded with natural light and perfectly oriented to capture sweeping, uninterrupted views of the Ionian Sea.

The villa offers six exquisitely designed sea-view suites, each showcasing timeless elegance and effortless sophistication. Every suite features a spacious en-suite bathroom and a private dressing room, thoughtfully curated to enhance comfort and privacy. Four of these refined suites are located in the Main House, while two additional suites are set within the exclusive Guest House, offering a heightened sense of seclusion.

A separate guest villa offers two further bedrooms with terraces, a stylish living room, a fully equipped kitchen, and an independent entrance, ensuring absolute privacy for guests or staff.

This exceptional residence features two heated infinity-edge swimming pools, an outdoor jacuzzi with panoramic sea views, a private spa with steam room, a state-of-the-art fitness area, and a private cinema. An elegant outdoor bar creates a refined atmosphere for entertaining, while the private dock offers direct sea access for yachts or tenders.

At this amazing villa, the essence of Mediterranean opulence comes to life through unparalleled natural beauty, thoughtful design, and exceptional hospitality.

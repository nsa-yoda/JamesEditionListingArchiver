## Description

Welcome to a Distinguished Estate — A European-Inspired Masterpiece Overlooking Swan Lake

Poised majestically on an elevated plateau above Swan Lake, this exceptional European-inspired estate spans 77 acres of premium farmland in the Township of Spallumcheen and Vernon. 
It offers panoramic views of pastoral landscapes and lake views.
Crafted with over 10,500 square feet of meticulously finished living space, this custom-built residence is a testament to timeless European elegance and artisanal craftsmanship. Step inside to discover masterful design elements—from intricate woodwork and soaring ceilings to grand-scale spaces curated for both refined living and memorable entertaining.

Property Highlights:

A gourmet chef’s kitchen featuring top-tier appliances and bespoke finishes

Formal and informal living areas, ideal for intimate gatherings or large-scale entertaining

A lavish primary suite with a spa-inspired ensuite and custom dressing room

Elevator access across multiple levels, complemented by two elegant staircases

A self-contained 2-bedroom guest suite for ultimate comfort and privacy

And so much more…

The estate’s land is currently utilized as a low-maintenance grain farm, preserving the natural beauty of the surroundings while offering exceptional agricultural potential—ideal for a private vineyard, equestrian pursuits, or bespoke retreat development.

Despite its peaceful rural setting, this estate is conveniently located just minutes from town and less than an hour from Kelowna International Airport. Nearby attractions include Predator Ridge Golf Resort, the turquoise waters of Kalamalka Lake, and the expansive Okanagan Lake.

For those who crave both luxury and adventure, the location is a four-season haven—offering skiing, hiking, biking, and access to world-renowned vineyards across the Okanagan Valley.

## Description

An absolute masterpiece designed by one of Vancouver's top architects, Frits de Vries. This sensational new Contemporary waterfront residence is situated majestically on a spectacular estate along North Vancouver’s most prized shoreline in Deep Cove.

This gorgeous oceanfront offers the finest in luxury finishings, incorporating a dramatic use of concrete, steel and wood highlighted by floor-to-ceiling sliding windows and accentuated ceilings providing breathtaking ocean views from all principal rooms. The sprawling, chevron shaped layout offers over 4,300 sq.ft. of living on 3 levels with entertainment-sized living and dining areas with double height ceilings, exotic millwork detail with wood burning fireplace and direct access to a sun filled patio with waterside lounge &amp; dining area. 

Additionally, the main floor features a stunning contemporary kitchen with top grade appliances, a large centre island for serving with breakfast bar, separate preparatory kitchen with pantry, an adjacent family room with media centre, elegant foyer &amp; powder room and laundry area.

The sophisticated quality, design &amp; engineering, together with the finest craftsmanship are evident throughout the entire residence. Many features include Home Automation, air-conditioning, hardwood &amp; tiled flooring, integrated lighting, music and security throughout the entire home.

Upstairs features a spectacular master bedroom suite with private view-side terrace, large dressing area and stunning five-piece ensuite bathroom, as well as, two additional guest bedrooms – both with private access to a tiled sun deck and shared 3 piece bath.

The lower level offers an oceanside entertainment area with media room, recreational area, gymnasium, 2 guest bedrooms, 2 magnificent temperature controlled &amp; glass enclosed wine cellars with natural rock face feature walls and a large wet bar providing direct access through a massive “Eclipse” door system opening to a sensational infinity-edge resort style swimming pool and intimate gardens with viewing area.

Descending to the oceanfront, waterside terraces provide additional sitting areas and access to your own private gangway &amp; dock, equipped to host a 50’ yacht and a multitude of ocean paraphernalia.

Additionally, this magnificent residence offers a level access with 3 car ocean view garage, 2 separate elevator cabs offering access to every breathtaking level, beautifully and professionally landscaped gardens with extensive night lighting, private courtyards, walkways and pathway to the private dock &amp; water’s edge. This exclusive property is World Class &amp; provides the ultimate in luxury oceanfront living!

## Description

Organic Winery boasting an historical farmstead, 31 hectares of vineyards, cellar and olive groves for sale in Montepulciano, Siena. This breathtaking estate offers an extraordinary opportunity to acquire a turnkey organic winery, with expansive vineyards, state-of-the-art winemaking facilities, and historical farmhouses. Located in one of Tuscany’s most iconic wine regions, Montepulciano, it combines tradition, modernity, and natural beauty—an idyllic setting for producing world-class wines and olive oil. Nestled in the heart of Tuscany's prestigious Montepulciano wine region, this exceptional organic winery estate offers an unparalleled opportunity to acquire a fully operational vineyard and winery, complete with charming historic farmhouses. The estate spans 48.5 hectares (119 acres) of stunning Tuscan countryside, with a combination of productive vineyards, olive groves, and arable land. The annual production reaches up to 100.000 bottles of the finest wines. The winery's production is primarily focused on Vino Nobile di Montepulciano DOCG, a wine steeped in tradition and craftsmanship. In addition, the estate produces a range of other exceptional wines, including Rosso di Montepulciano, as well as the award-winning Super Tuscans. The property includes two beautifully restored stone farmhouses, providing a total of 742 square meters (7,986 sq ft) of residential space, as well as extensive winemaking and ageing facilities covering 1,639 square meters (17,642 sq ft). The estate is in excellent condition, offering the ideal combination of tradition and modernity in a picturesque setting. This unique property offers a rare opportunity to acquire a fully integrated, sustainable wine and olive oil estate in one of Tuscany's most sought-after regions.

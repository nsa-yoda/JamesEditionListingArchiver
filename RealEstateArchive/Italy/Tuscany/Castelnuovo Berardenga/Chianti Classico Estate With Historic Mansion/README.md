## Description

102-hectare/252-acre estate for sale in the heart of Chianti Classico with vineyards, winemaking facility and historic mansion with farmstead. Near the village of Vagliagli, just 10 km north from Siena, the estate covers 102 hectares/252 acres, between the municipalities of Radda in Chianti and Castelnuovo Berardenga, made of rolling hills, valleys and a 2.4 acres (1 ha) private lake. The vineyards cover 70.4 acres (28.5 hectares), with right for an additional 7 hectares (17 acres) to be replanted, and each has its own unique terroir: a combination of soil, altitude and exposure, resulting in wines of extraordinary complexity. Currently under organic conversion since 2021, with final certification planned for 2024/2025, it produces some 130,000 bottles of fine Chianti Classico DOCG (4 labels) and IGT Toscana (6 labels), all of which have been awarded for their authentic expression of terroir with excellent scores obtained from JamesSuckling, Wine Hunter and Decanter. The wines are also featured in the most renowned wine guides such as Slow Wine, Veronelli, Weinwisser and Bibenda. More than 2,000 square meters (21527 sq ft) on three levels are dedicated to the winemaking facility, equipped with cargo lifts, convenient access for trucks, air conditioning, basement cellar with ageing rooms and barrels, from steel to wood. The centuries of history of this place are preserved in the manor house, built on the walls of a medieval fortress that still preserves its ramparts, some towers and the stone bridge dating back to the 1400s. After centuries of battles it was later transformed into a luxury mansion where the Tuscan aristocracy spent the summer months thanks to the fantastic Chianti Classico region microclimate. Around the villa are the farm buildings in typical Tuscan style. One has been recovered with 8 suites, one as tasting rooms, while both the villa and the other buildings need overall recovery, total area of approx. 2500 square meters, with excellent potential to develop both a wine luxury resort or a private residence, ideal for receptions, entertainments, events and vacations surrounded by vineyards and nature. In a discreet and private corner of Tuscany, accessible by a 750-meter private driveway, close to stores, historic centers, and only 45 minutes south of Florence.

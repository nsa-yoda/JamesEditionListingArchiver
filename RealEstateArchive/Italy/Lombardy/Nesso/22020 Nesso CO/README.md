## Description

Exclusively listed with BHHS Palazzo Estate - just steps from the shores of Lake Como, with rare direct access to the waters, we are pleased to present a scenic, bright villa with garden in Nesso. This charming village is one of the most characteristic lakeside destinations located between Como and Bellagio.

The property represents a rare opportunity for those seeking an independent villa with lake views, direct water access, outdoor living spaces, and a setting with nature. The coveted access to Lake Como is particularly appreciated by those who wish to enjoy Lake Como year-round, practice water sports, or simply spend relaxing moments by the water.

The villa, of ca. 205 sqm (2,207 sqft.), is arranged over two levels, surrounded by a private garden of ca. 450 sqm (4,844 sqft.). The main level features an entrance hall and hallway leading to the sleeping quarters, comprising three bedrooms, each enjoying lake views, and two windowed bathrooms. The living area includes an open-concept kitchen and a large, light-filled living room enhanced by expansive windows overlooking Lake Como. Particularly beautiful are the two panoramic lake-view terraces, offering ideal spaces to enjoy the surrounding scenery throughout the day.

The lower level accommodates two additional bedrooms and a bathroom. Outdoors, the villa benefits from a covered porch with a dedicated outdoor dining area and kitchen, in addition to the private garden, creating inviting spaces for enjoying the lakeside lifestyle. An additional feature of significant value is two private parking spaces exclusively serving the property. Additional public parking conveniently is located nearby.

Nesso is one of the most charming villages on Lake Como, renowned for its historic 'Orrido' gorge, scenic walking paths, and authentic atmosphere preserved through the years. The location allows both Como and Bellagio to be reached in ca. 20 to 25 minutes. The area also offers a wide range of recreational opportunities, including boating, water sports, hiking, nature trails, and acclaimed waterfront restaurants.

The property is located ca. 1 hour from Milan Malpensa Airport and ca. 1 hour and 15 minutes from both Milan Linate Airport and Lugano, providing convenient access to major Italian and international destinations.

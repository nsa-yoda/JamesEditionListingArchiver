## Description

This superbly renovated Art Deco style villa in Cap d’Ail offers the highest luxury finishes and a magical panoramic sea view. Located between Monaco and Cap Ferrat this is the ultimate French Riviera lifestyle villa. Access to the Plage Mala private beach and also easy access from your yacht adds to the unique features of this property. The villa offers– 7 bedrooms (4 guest bedrooms and 3 principal bedrooms with separate wardrobes, all with en suite bathrooms and terraces) – Lobby, living room, TV room – library, dining room, kitchen, cold and wine cellar – SPA, terrace bar and party terrace, 2 jacuzzi and swimming pool – garage 2 cars and parking 12 cars, security room – Mooring – direct sea access. A glass lift with panoramic views leads from the parking on the upper floor directly to the entertaining level. The interiors are extremely bright, with large double French doors offering panoramic sea views, the decor is elegant, with cream marble floors and neutral coloured suede designer furniture. The villa is equipped with the latest technology. It includes Plasma TV’s in each room, video and DVD system, Sky satellite, Crestron music system through the whole villa and garden, villa-wide internet access, Infra-red alarm system on each window plus security cameras, electric shutters and climate control throughout.There is a fully equipped gym, a hammam and a jet shower room. The ‘Boffi’ professional kitchen is equipped with a cold room (equivalent to 6 fridges!), a Japanese hob for tepanyaki cuisine, regular hob, plate warmers, covers for 24, two dishwashers, two ovens, microwave and coffee machine and Viking wine coolers for 200 bottles. There is also an oversized Viking gas BBQ off the kitchen.

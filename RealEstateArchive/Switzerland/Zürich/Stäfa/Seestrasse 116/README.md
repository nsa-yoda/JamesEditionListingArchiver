## Description

This exclusive villa in a privileged lakeside setting combines first-class craftsmanship and premium finishes with a spectacular private wellness retreat – an exceptional residence for those with the highest expectations of location, quality, and lifestyle.
Stäfa on Lake Zurich is one of the most sought-after residential communities on the Upper Gold Coast. Sunny vineyards, an attractive tax rate (83%), and an outstanding quality of life make this municipality of around 15,000 residents particularly desirable. Shopping facilities, schools, and excellent restaurants are all nearby, while Zurich, Rapperswil, and Zurich Airport are easily accessible, including by train.
Seestrasse 116 is among the most exclusive addresses in the region. Hidden behind mature hedges lies a private paradise offering uninterrupted 180° lake views, a stunning mountain panorama, and all-day sunshine.
Built in 2011 by the renowned architect Senn, the villa combines the timeless elegance of a classic country estate with contemporary luxury. Generous, light-filled living spaces, exquisite materials, and a harmonious architectural concept define the living experience. Highlights include the approximately 90 m² main living area, elegant bedroom suites with en-suite bathrooms, and additional private rooms on the top floor. A passenger lift conveniently connects all levels.
A truly unique feature is the lower ground floor, dedicated to an impressive 200 m² wellness oasis. It includes an indoor swimming pool with counter-current system, Jacuzzi, sauna, fitness area, massage room, lounge, and a sheltered outdoor relaxation space. Additional amenities include guest accommodation, a utility room, a walk-in cold storage room, and an underground garage with car lift providing space for five vehicles. Technical and storage rooms are discreetly located on the second basement level.
An exclusive lakeside retreat in one of the most spectacular settings on Lake Zurich.

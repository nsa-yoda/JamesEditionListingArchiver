## Description

Discover unparalleled luxury on Waiheke Island in this private secluded gated estate.One of New Zealand’s most prestigious residences masterfully engineered with an extraordinarily high level of construction featuring premium imported materials throughout. Exquisite quartzite bench tops, sub-floor heating, a grand entrance soaring to a near 6 meter stud height, 4 meter ceilings in living areas that emphasise majestic volume and scale. Host intimate gatherings with friends in your very own expansive underground cellar and bar, a true entertainers dream.Enjoy your own private vineyard managed by Stonyridge, one of Waiheke’s premier wineries, with further bare land awaiting your decision to possibly extend the vineyard. Explore 1.6km of impeccably landscaped private walking trails with stunning vistas that provides exclusive access to various points on the estate to relax and enjoy. Exclusive access to the secluded bay frequented by dolphins and perfect for all water sports and exceptional fishing is on your doorstep.The separate guest accommodation provides the extra space for those coming to enjoy all that you take for granted and space allows for further accommodation to be developed if you desire.Revel in spectacular north-facing views, sheltered and private all finished to a level that must be viewed to be appreciated. Located less than 10 minutes’ drive from world class wineries and restaurants, seamless connectivity via ferry access on and off the island to the city or simply arrive by helicopter to your own private helipad.For the elite few who seek sanctuary beyond compare, this Waiheke waterfront estate delivers uncompromising luxury, and a legacy worthy of generations.

## Description

Recognized by Forbes as one of the Top Ten Exclusive and Expensive Gated Communities in the United States, this is a rare chance to own a fully upgraded estate in one of Boca Raton's most coveted and secure waterfront enclaves.

Stunning waterfront estate located in Boca Raton's ultra-exclusive, guard-gated Sanctuary neighborhood - one of the safest and most prestigious deep-water communities in the nation. This newly updated residence showcases over $1,000,000 in recent upgrades, delivering a refined blend of architectural elegance, modern luxury, and seamless indoor-outdoor living.

Step into a grand foyer with soaring 30'+ awe-inspiring ceilings, dramatic window walls, and expansive open living spaces filled with natural light. The home features a designer entertainment bar, billiards room, gym room, chef's kitchen with top-tier appliances, media room, private elevator, and multiple living and dining spaces ideal for both family life and sophisticated entertaining.

The luxury primary suite is a resort retreat of its own - featuring dual bathrooms, separate his &amp; hers closets, private sitting areas, and serene water views. Guest suites are spacious and private, each offering its own upscale finishes.

Enjoy the South Florida lifestyle from a resort-style pool, lush landscaping, and a private dock with deep-water access, perfect for yachting. Additional enhancements include updated flooring, custom lighting, refined millwork, elevated finishes throughout, and meticulous maintenance in every area of the home.

Located in The Sanctuary - an elite yachting community with 24/7 armed gate security, water patrol, and a private marina - this residence provides unmatched peace of mind and exclusivity. Just minutes from Boca Raton's private airport, I-95, Boca Country Club, Mizner Park, world-class dining, pristine beaches, and premier shopping.

The Sanctuary HOA covers:
~ Security: 24/7 armed guard gated entry, roving patrols, monitored access for both land and waterfront.
~ Exclusive Amenities: Private 20-slip marina, tennis/basketball court, playground, and a 27-acre bird/wildlife preserve.
~ Common Area Maintenance: Care of all community landscaping, roads, and shared common spaces.
~ Concierge Services: Additional personal assistance for residents is provided as part of HOA membership
~ Privacy: High standards for resident safety and seclusion, contributing to the community's reputation as one of the most secure and exclusive in the area.

## Description

A rare opportunity to own a private island with 50 acres and only minutes from Key West. So many possibilities, build your own Equestrian Estate, ’’The Southernmost stables’’. This property is an excellent place to host large events like concerts, weddings, farm to table / wine tastings, or possibly therapeutic programs or retreats. Beautiful beach areas. Easy to get to, as the island has access to the mainland via a ½ mile land bridge. The property is also located next to one of the largest mega yacht marinas and close to two upscale hotels. The owners used to run a small farm, bee keeping, music events, and kids summer camps on the island. One of the few places with untouched nature left in the Florida Keys.

## Description

Welcome to Shotwell Farm, a 172-acre equestrian estate and recognized Jefferson County landmark. A tree-lined drive leads to a Southern Living style main home with a detached garage and 2-bedroom apartment. A circular drive serves the residence, while a separate entrance directs visitors to the equestrian facilities.The main barn spans 37,857 square feet and features a center-stall layout, generator, reception area, two offices, laundry, and three staff apartments (one on the first floor, two above). Beyond reception are 29 stalls, a wash stall, and a 80’ x 300’ indoor arena at the core of the barn.Nearby, a 12,000-square-foot building includes a dedicated breeding area and a 2,520-square-foot maintenance shop with 200-amp service and water. An additional 39 stalls bring the total to 68. Two 6,000-square-foot structures at the rear provide hay and equipment storage; the hay barn includes a 16’ x 100’ lean-to.Pastures are enclosed by 18,000 linear feet of four-rail blackboard fencing and 7,500 feet of top-rail fencing with diamond wire. The farm is well-equipped with run-in sheds, ample storage, and 29 automatic waterers.Also on the property is a 1,200-square-foot split-level manager’s home with 3 bedrooms, 2 baths and an attached 2-car garage.Beautiful rolling pastured, convenient to both Charles Town racetrack and Virginia.

## Description

Located in the prestigious Two Rivers community, this stunning gated Mediterranean-inspired estate offers exceptional privacy, wellness, and a connection to nature, all within easy reach of Nashville and Franklin. Set on 13 usable acres with direct backyard access to the West Harpeth River, enjoy kayaking, tubing, fishing, or peaceful afternoons along the water.

The thoughtfully designed residence offers 8,970 sq ft of refined living space, plus a 3,341 sq ft attached multipurpose facility currently used for basketball and fitness training. This versatile space is ideal for a private gym, indoor sport court, wellness studio, recreation area, entertainment venue, or a car enthusiast's collection.

Outdoor living is equally impressive with a newly added heated Winborn pool, oversized fire bowl, and outdoor kitchen creating a resort-style setting surrounded by mature trees and expansive grounds. The authentic stucco exterior is highlighted by a striking imported centerpiece fountain. Inside, natural travertine flooring, hickory hardwoods, generous entertaining spaces, and timeless architectural details create warmth and elegance throughout.

The main level features three bedrooms, a private office with its own entrance, and a flexible wellness room. Upstairs offers two en-suite bedrooms plus bonus spaces ideal for media, fitness, play, or creative use.

Additional highlights include a five-car garage, mature landscaping, and impeccably maintained grounds. Add a barn, bring the horses, and create the quintessential Tennessee equestrian property where you can truly plant roots.

A rare opportunity to own a private riverfront retreat combining land, recreation, wellness, and architectural beauty just minutes from Downtown Franklin, Cool Springs, Green Hills, and Brentwood.

## Description

Set across nearly 400 acres in the heart of Vermont, Monsalvat Farm is a rare legacy estate where extraordinary architecture, natural beauty, and complete privacy come together in remarkable harmony. Surrounded by rolling pastures, forests, orchards, spring-fed ponds, and sweeping mountain views, the property offers a world of its own—quiet, timeless, and deeply connected to the landscape. A private gated entrance leads across a stone bridge over a tranquil pond, setting the tone for an estate designed to inspire from the moment of arrival. At its center is The Sanctuary, an architectural masterpiece crafted with exceptional attention to detail. Built from hand-laid stone, timber, granite, and reclaimed materials, the residence blends old-world craftsmanship with modern comfort. Soaring cathedral ceilings, dramatic stone fireplaces, a circular library, wine cellar, music and media spaces, and beautifully curated gathering rooms create a home that feels both grand and welcoming. Beyond the main residence, the estate includes Gilbert’s Cottage, a charming stone guest house; a restored farmhouse with multiple residences; The Lodge; a historic barn and stable; and a lakeside gazebo complete with a fireplace and entertaining spaces. Miles of trails wind throughout the property, connecting open fields, wooded hillsides, gardens, ponds, and scenic overlooks. Whether envisioned as a private family retreat, multigenerational compound, equestrian estate, or legacy holding, Monsalvat Farm offers an extraordinary opportunity to own one of New England’s most significant private properties. Located in Barnard, Vermont, Monsalvat Farm is a place of rare beauty, permanence, and distinction—created to be enjoyed for generations to come.&nbsp;

## Description

Set directly above the golden sand in Ha‘ena on Kaua‘i’s North Shore, this contemporary beachfront home offers immediate access to the beach in a quiet, naturally sheltered setting. Reef-protected water lies just beyond the lanai, with ridgelines behind, creating a location that feels open yet protected.

The open, one-level floor plan supports easy, everyday living. The home connects indoor and outdoor spaces through covered and shaded lanais finished in ipe decking that extend the living areas toward the shoreline. The design supports a relaxed way of living—morning walks on the beach, time in the water, outdoor meals, and evenings spent listening to the ocean.

Interior finishes are natural and durable, including reclaimed teak cabinetry, wide-plank walnut flooring, honed black basalt countertops, and custom millwork. Rooms are spacious and well proportioned throughout. The primary suite is set apart with private lanai access and a bathroom that includes a soaking tub and walk-in steam shower.

The kitchen is centrally located and designed for daily use and entertaining, with a large island, Viking cooktop, Sub-Zero refrigerator, and open living spaces that flow into a media room. Two additional bedrooms with en suite bathrooms provide flexibility for guests or extended stays.

Additional features include a brand new split-zoned air-conditioning system, an outdoor shower, whole-house water filtration, integrated audio systems, solar photovoltaic system with Tesla PowerWall technology; copper gutters with rain chains; gated entry; and an attached two-car garage. The property has been consistently cared for.

Just beyond Hanalei, Ha‘ena is known for limited development and long-term stewardship. The home is close to Tunnels Beach; Ke‘e Beach; Lumaha‘i Beach; Limahuli Garden; and the Nā Pali Coast trails while remaining removed from traffic and crowds.

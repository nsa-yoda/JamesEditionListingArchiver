## Description

There is oceanfront property - and there is exceptional oceanfront living. From this North Hawaii estate home on twenty plus acres overlooking a half-mile of dramatic coastline to the west and a gulch with a small stony beach on its eastern boundary, you can: 
? watch sunrise and sunset, moonrise and moonset, and frequent rainbows 
? enjoy over one hundred eighty degrees of ocean view, Maui rising across the channel, and the Kohala mountains and historic valleys sheltering from behind 
? watch whales breaching up close in season, cruise ships passing in the distance, 'iwa and koa'e kea soaring overhead 
? glimpse cows and horses grazing in nearby pastures 
? feel the serenity of the rural North Kohala location, with restaurants, boutiques, services and farm-to-table agriculture only a few miles away.

The gracious home of nearly ten thousand square feet was built in 2005 but has been almost completely renovated inside and out since 2018. Offering both warmth and privacy for extended family or visiting friends, its generous proportions and thoughtful floor plan with multiple living spaces are equally perfect for entertaining at scale. The owners have hosted magical musical evenings for dozens of guests from the community.

The kitchen, main living areas, and one guest bedroom are on the second floor. Ascend the grand spiral staircase from the foyer or bring groceries directly from garage to kitchen and butler's pantry via a commercial grade elevator.

The primary bedroom suite, three additional guest bedrooms, secondary living room and lanai are located on the ground floor. The primary bedroom features a seating area, an oversized walk-in closet with custom cabinetry, and a large en suite bathroom with ocean view tub and shower. The attached private lanai area features seating and punee (daybeds), an outdoor shower, and a hot tub to enjoy on starry nights.

Furnishings and art were carefully selected to enhance the sense of place; most are available for sale.

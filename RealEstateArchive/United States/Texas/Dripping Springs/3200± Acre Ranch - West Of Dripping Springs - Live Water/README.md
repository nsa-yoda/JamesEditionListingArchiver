## Description

This wildlife exempt 3,200± acre ranch sits just west of Dripping Springs—about 45 minutes from downtown Austin and within 30 minutes of Texas’ top vineyard region between Johnson City and Fredericksburg. Ranches of this size rarely come available this close to Austin and still offer this level of privacy.

The property has amazing water features, including 1.5 miles of spring-fed live water creek that runs year-round, even through droughts. The creek is dammed up three times, forming large lakes and stocked ponds that add beauty and recreation to the land.

The views across the rolling hills are spectacular, and the pastures are open, clean, and ideal for cattle, horses, or wildlife. The land is improved and well maintained, with quality roads, fencing, and structures already in place.

The main home sits on a hilltop and is over 7,000 sq ft, custom-built with incredible long-range views. It includes a negative-edge pool and a large outdoor living area for relaxing or entertaining. Two smaller rustic guest homes sit close to the creek below, keeping the original ranch charm.

The ranch encompasses four school districts—the majority falling within Dripping Springs ISD and Johnson City ISD, with smaller portions lying in Wimberley ISD and Blanco ISD.

The ranch also includes a large equipment barn and a full equestrian facility with stables and paddocks—well suited for equestrian enthusiasts.

Wildlife is abundant and unpressured, having not been hunted for many years, with a healthy population of whitetail deer, turkey, dove, and pheasants. Included on the ranch are deer feeders, turkey feeders, and food plots, supporting a strong habitat for a wide variety of wildlife. There is also great fishing throughout the ranch in the 10+ acre lake, additional smaller lakes, and the large bodies of water created by the creek dams.

The property is completely high-fenced with four different access points and is under a wildlife management program.

The property would present as an excellent high end luxury development opportunity. Has also been measured out for a potential 4,340 ft airstrip.

A smaller 855± acre option with the improvements and live water is available separately.

## Description

Welcome to Mustang Creek Ranch, a remarkable 855-acre property tucked into the rolling hills just west of Dripping Springs, Texas. Potential additional acreage - contact listing agent for details. Located approximately 45 minutes from downtown Austin, this rare live-water ranch seamlessly blends the natural beauty of the Hill Country with comfort, privacy, and thoughtful improvements throughout.

The property features nearly a mile and a half of Mustang Creek, a clear, spring-fed waterway that runs year-round, even in dry months. The creek winds through valleys, past large oak trees and limestone bluffs, and has been dammed in several areas to create deep, clean lakes ideal for fishing, kayaking, and swimming. The land itself is a mix of open pastures, wooded ridges, and high points that showcase sweeping views of the surrounding countryside.

Sitting on one of the ranch’s most impressive hilltops is a 7,000-square-foot custom home designed for both comfort and function. The house has six bedrooms, seven bathrooms, and a layout built for hosting family and friends. Recent updates include new hardwood floors, a modern kitchen with updated appliances, and a Savant home system that seamlessly integrates lighting, sound, and security. Outside, a negative-edge pool overlooks the hills, offering a quiet spot to take in sunset views that stretch for miles.

A private, gated entrance leads to a paved one-mile driveway, ensuring total privacy from the road. The entire ranch is high-fenced and managed to benefit wildlife. You’ll find a large pond near the entry, along with 10 deer feeders, six turkey feeders, and six established food plots that attract native and exotic game throughout the year.

Closer to the creek, the property includes a rustic guest house, a foreman’s home, a practical equestrian setup, and a large equipment barn for ranch operations. Whether you’re spending the weekend fishing along Mustang Creek, hunting with family, or simply enjoying the quiet of Hill Country life, this property has been carefully shaped to offer both adventure and relaxation.

Mustang Creek Ranch is more than just land; it’s a piece of Texas that brings together water, wildlife, and wide-open space in one of the most desirable areas of the state. With its location near Dripping Springs, Johnson City, Wimberley, and the wineries of Fredericksburg, it’s close to everything yet feels a world apart.

## Description

Rare opportunity to own an incredible custom home in one of the most sought after locations on Balboa Island, positioned along North Bayfront with sweeping Bay views and seamless indoor outdoor living throughout. This spectacular custom home was designed by Brandon Architects, as well as Tiffany Harris Design. A spacious front patio with fireplace and custom stonework opens to sliding bifold doors and an expansive great room, where the custom stonework seamlessly carries from the exterior into the interior, creating a cohesive architectural feel across the main living spaces and fireplace. The great room flows into a dining area and a well appointed kitchen with a large island, bar seating, premium appliances, and a dedicated wine pantry, all oriented toward the water. The second level offers two guest bedrooms, a bath, and a laundry room, while the primary suite is set apart as a true retreat with a private waterfront balcony, fireplace, and wide opening glass doors that bring the Bay directly into the space. The suite is complemented by two stunning bathrooms, including one with a soaking tub, walk-in shower, toilet, and generous walk-in closet with custom California Closets built-ins, and a second bathroom with its own shower, toilet, and custom California Closets built-in wardrobe storage. The third level is designed for entertaining, featuring a covered loggia with kitchenette and seating area, along with an open deck with fireplace, all positioned to take in expansive views of the Bay and beyond. Additional features include elevator access to all three levels, highlighted by a unique glass ceiling and skylight that create a dramatic elevator experience, integrated Control4 audio and smart lighting throughout, and a two-car garage, offering a rare combination of scale, design, and premier Island living.

Welcome to The Apex of Billionaire's Row, Bel Air

Mornings at 677 Nimes Road begin in a world above the clouds, where the soft hum of Bel Air's canyon breeze meets the silent, automated retreat of floor-to-ceiling glass. Positioned on the most coveted stretch of 'Billionaire's Row,' this architectural titan commands 320 feet of majestic frontage, offering a lifestyle reserved for those who view the world from the summit. It is a residence designed not just to be lived in, but to be experienced as a series of grand, cinematic moments-a place where the golden hour of the Los Angeles skyline becomes your personal, nightly backdrop.

The pulse of the home is found in its sweeping, open-concept gallery spaces, where soaring ceilings and dramatic linear fireplaces create an atmosphere of effortless power. Whether you are hosting a high-stakes gala across the three gourmet kitchens or enjoying a quiet vintage in the glass-enclosed wine cellar, the home transitions seamlessly from a social epicenter to a private sanctuary. Outside, the world-class infinity pool creates a glass-like horizon that merges with the city lights below, while the sprawling entertainment deck-engineered to perfection on a foundation of 110 concrete piles-provides a literal and metaphorical platform for the highest level of hospitality.

A museum-grade sanctuary for the automotive enthusiast. Beyond the executive 5-car garage lies a subterranean gallery and expansive grounds capable of accommodating a 50-vehicle collection, depending on size. This is more than a garage; it is a high-capacity vault for world-class collections, offering a level of utility rarely seen in Bel Air. Every corner of the 19,348-square-foot interior is dedicated to the art of the retreat, from the state-of-the-art theater to the holistic wellness wing, featuring a private salon and both wet and dry saunas that rival the world's most elite spas.

The day culminates in the primary suite-a masterwork of seclusion featuring vaulted ceilings, dual boutique-style closets, and two lavish spa baths that offer a sculptural escape from the city's vibrant energy. Connected by a statement staircase and a commercial-grade elevator, every level of this home reinforces a sense of permanence and prestige. 677 Nimes Road is a legacy asset, a fortress of glass and steel standing at the absolute apex of Bel Air's legendary landscape.

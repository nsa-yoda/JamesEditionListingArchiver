## Description

Set within the highly coveted Riviera Palisades - an exclusive neighborhood largely unaffected by the fires of 2025 - 1509 Amalfi Drive offers a rare balance of privacy, scale, and natural beauty across more than half an acre of lush, tranquil grounds. Taken down to the framing in 2024 and creatively expanded and reimagined by the renowned Claudia Janet, the home unfolds with understated elegance behind private gates. A discreet entry opens to light-filled interiors and a seamless connection to the surrounding landscape. Expansive walls of glass perfectly frame sweeping ocean and canyon views, inviting the outdoors in and creating an effortless indoor-outdoor flow throughout. Natural materials, soaring ceilings, and warm oak floors establish a sense of calm, while the open layout is designed for both everyday living and elevated entertaining. At the heart of the home, the bespoke Italian kitchen by renowned D. I. Group pairs refined design with cutting-edge function. Hand-finished Chukum countertops, top-tier appliances, and a hyper-filtered reverse osmosis alkaline water system offering still, hot, cold, or carbonated mineral water at the touch of a button, reflect a thoughtful approach to both form and function. Each bedroom suite is designed as a private retreat, offering generous scale, clean lines, and a sense of quiet separation. Radiant-heated en-suite bathrooms add comfort, while curated spaces throughout the home emphasize balance, privacy, and ease. The grounds are equally compelling and designed as an extension of the living experience. Amenities include a Rejuvenation Spa with contrast therapy, cold plunge, and hot sauna; a glass-paneled plunge pool set against the landscape; and a meditation garden known as Inspiration Point. Meandering paths, a private petit vineyard with adjacent wine terrace, gentle waterfalls, turtle pond and a serene natural setting create an environment that feels both expansive and intimate. A detached guest casita provides additional flexibility for hosting or extended family stays, thoughtfully positioned to capture the property’s breathtaking views. Moments from Riviera Country Club, Brentwood Country Mart, Santa Monica and Beverly Hills, this is a property that offers both retreat and connection to the best Los Angeles has to offer. 1509 Amalfi Drive is not simply a home, it’s a place defined by privacy, design, and a deep sense of calm.

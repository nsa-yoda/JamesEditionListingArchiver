## Description

Just minutes from Yountville, this 6.7 acre Napa Valley compound is a masterclass in design, engineering, and quiet luxury. Conceived by acclaimed architect Holder Parlette and built by renowned building scientist Karim Allana, the 8,600 sq ft estate delivers 6 bedrooms, 7 full baths, and 3 half baths with a level of precision rarely seen in residential construction. Surrounded by 4 acres of professionally farmed vineyards and framed by sweeping 360-degree views of the Mayacamas and Vaca ranges, the setting is both dramatic and serene. A separate 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath guest house complements the main residence, while resort-caliber amenitiesincluding a 60x20 pool with spa and an outdoor kitchen and dining pavilioncreate a seamless indoor-outdoor lifestyle. Every detail is intentional. Designed with off-grid capability and commercial-grade systems, the home pairs advanced performance with elevated design. Two luxurious primary suites, European Reynaers sliding door systems, artisan finishes, and a 12.5kW solar array are integrated into a fully smart, future-forward residence. Rare, refined, and exceptionally engineered this is Napa Valley at its most sophisticated, where sustainability meets undeniable luxury.

## Description

Perched atop one of Napa Valley’s most storied ridge lines, 3000 Mt Veeder Road is a rare and remarkable estate in the heart of the prestigious Mount Veeder AVA. Encompassing approx. 130 ultra-private acres, this world-class compound blends bold architecture with elite viticulture in a resort-caliber setting. At its center is a striking 5,067 sq ft main residence, a 4-bedroom showpiece of Art Deco glamour and Modernist sophistication. Completed in 1988, the home features grand living spaces, a library/office, and a spacious gym, all positioned to capture breathtaking vistas. A dramatic pool terrace leads to a 5-story, 2-bedroom 1,380 sq ft guest tower, believed to be Napa Valley’s tallest residential structure with 360 panoramic views from its rooftop deck. The estate also includes multiple terraces, a 2-bedroom caretaker’s residence, and 8 acres of premium Cabernet Sauvignon vines yielding approx. 500 cases annually. 3000 Mount Veeder Road is more than a home: it’s a statement of scale, solitude, and sophistication, offering a once-in-a-lifetime lifestyle in one of Napa’s most iconic settings.

## Description

Introducing an extraordinary opportunity nestled on a private road in the esteemed North Tustin hills within the award-winning Tustin Unified School District. This stunning 7,100-square-foot architectural gem has graced the pages of notable publications, including ”Architectural Styles of Orange County, ” and encapsulates a lavish ”Beverly Hills” lifestyle with sweeping 180-degree panoramic views that stretch from the Pacific Ocean to the iconic Los Angeles skyline. This expansive estate boasts 7 bedrooms and 6 bathrooms and has undergone a substantial two-year, multi-million-dollar renovation, ensuring that every detail reflects modern luxury. The centerpiece of this sophisticated home is the beautifully redesigned second-floor chef’s kitchen, featuring an impressive 11.5-foot quartz center island, dual dishwashers, microwaves, and sinks, all complemented by elegant new flooring. The family room is perfect for relaxation with its remote-controlled electric fireplace and a 98-inch TV. The nearby living room shares the same captivating views, enhancing the overall allure. Ascend to the third floor to discover a personal sanctuary, highlighted by a primary suite complete with a 25-foot open-concept closet, custom beauty stations, ample storage, and a unique John Wick style ”Battle Bunker” closet, perfect for wardrobe and firearm enthusiasts. The Las Vegas spa-inspired bathroom boasts a glass door champagne closet, dual rainforest shower heads, pressure washers, and a luxurious jacuzzi tub. For those who love to entertain, this level features a dedicated bar and lounge area, complete with a movable pool table, a dance floor, a karaoke system with a 98-inch TV, mood lighting, and a private wine closet. Prioritizing wellness, the first floor includes a fully remodeled studio bedroom that houses a new modern bathroom and a sauna. This studio bedroom can also be used a multi-generational room or rental since it boasts a full-size kitchen, laundry area and can be accessed from outside or within the home. Outdoor amenities are just as impressive, showcasing a completely renovated pool, brand new outdoor kitchen, fresh landscaping with LED lighting, and a cozy outdoor hammock area under the palm trees for relaxation and of course more impressive views. Don’t miss your chance to own this unparalleled estate, where luxury meets comfort in the foothills of North Tustin.

## Description

Originally a turn of the century horse racing track named Field Point Park, the prestigious Field Point Circle Association is now one of Greenwich’s most coveted guard-gated enclaves. Distinguished by its exclusive address, this grandly proportioned French-inspired manor of 9,565 +/- square feet combines stately elegance on a level 2.2 acres with exquisite privacy and a pool with spa. Clad in limestone with a slate roof, the six bedroom, seven full and two bathroom residence is is distinguished by a flexible floor plan, offering a primary suite option on both the first and second floors. Principal entertaining spaces—an elegant living room, gracious dining room, and refined library—are enhanced by high ceilings and oversized windows that flood the home with natural light.&nbsp;Opening to a grand rotunda with a double-height curved staircase, the marble foyer is the intersection between the east and west wings of the house. On the east side, an elegant formal dining room adjoins the butler’s pantry and opens to the generously sized kitchen with pantry, breakfast area and powder room. There is an attached 3-car garage in this wing, with a laundry room. Formal rooms in the west wing include an airy living room with fireplace and cozy library with fireplace and concealed wet bar which opens through French doors to the private backyard terrace. The ground floor primary suite with dressing room and full bathroom also overlooks the peaceful backyard. There is another first floor en-suite bedroom across the hall. A great room that is perfect for entertaining adjoins the full pool bathroom, kitchenette, bar and dining area which serves as a “pool house” and opens to the main terrace and pool with spa. The great room was originally an indoor pool and the pool is intact below the floor and can be put back into use.Screened by greenery for ultimate privacy, the secluded backyard with verdant level lawn and pool with spa completes this idyllic retreat perfect for all lifestyles.The Field Point Circle Association consists of only 27 homes and offers 24-hour security, while its wide, tree-lined boulevards maintain its glamorous, Gilded Age legacy. In a treasured coastal location, this historic neighborhood offers a compelling mix of proximity to downtown Greenwich, as well as easy access to New York City vis Interstate 95 or the Metro North train.

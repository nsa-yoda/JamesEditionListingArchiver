## Description

Reimagined and rebuilt with historic soul, this Old Town home sits only steps from the Town Run, with exceptional access to skiing at Park City Mountain and to Main Street’s restaurants, galleries, and the Town Lift Plaza. Few properties offer this blend of ski-in convenience and walkable mountain-town charm. Inside, radiant in-floor heating and cooling with a warm board system pairs with redundant VRF heat pumps and zoned climate control, so every room feels just right, year-round. Rift-sawn white oak floors, cabinetry, and custom doors run throughout, complemented by walnut accents, open-tread staircases, and Lutron lighting and shades that set the mood at the touch of a button. The kitchen is built for gathering—walnut cabinetry, quartz countertops, a Miele appliance suite with induction range and double oven, and an island made for entertaining. The great room’s gas fireplace and accordion doors open straight to the back patio, where a full snow-melt system keeps the driveway, stairs, porch, and balcony clear all season. Four ensuite bedroom suites include custom walnut closets and spa-like baths with Toto Washlets, floating vanities, and Axor and Hansgrohe fixtures. The primary suite adds its own balcony, dual vanity, and multiple shower heads. Downstairs, a media room and Walnut-clad wet bar are made for movie nights and gathering, while a mudroom, loft, and flex space add everyday flexibility. Additional highlights include a zinc metal roof with snow retention, whole-home water filtration, an HRV air filtration system, and a radiant-heated garage with epoxy flooring. Staging by Empire Crest Interiors. This is historic Old Town living, elevated.

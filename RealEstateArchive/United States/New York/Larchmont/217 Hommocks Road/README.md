## Description

Welcome to Villa Cheri, a spectacular waterfront estate at 217 Hommocks Road, where timeless elegance meets extraordinary coastal living along the Long Island Sound. Set on 1.38 beautifully landscaped acres, this exceptional property features sweeping water views, a private dock, pool, cabana, charming waterfront gazebo, and private beach creating an idyllic setting for both grand entertaining and serene everyday living.
From the moment you enter the grand foyer, sweeping water views draw you through glass doors framed by a dramatic fireplace and coffered ceilings—setting the stage for a home designed to celebrate both luxury and landscape. The magnificent chef’s kitchen is a true showpiece, centered around an oversized waterfall marble island and equipped with Gaggenau and L’Atelier appliances, radiant heated stone floors, and a charming breakfast room with a custom circular banquet. Doors open to covered porches and an outdoor kitchen, seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor entertaining.
Elegant formal rooms include a dining room with coffered ceilings and dentil molding, a gracious living room with fireplace, piano alcove, and doors to covered porch, and a sophisticated library with lacquer-painted walls and built-in shelving. A stunning billiards room with lacquered paneled walls, wet bar, beverage refrigerator, and glass-and-brass shelving creates the perfect setting for evening gatherings, while the inviting family room with fireplace and sliding ladder bookcases opens to the porch for relaxed waterfront enjoyment.
A dramatic staircase leads to the second floor where the luxurious primary suite offers a private sitting area, balcony with water views, a custom dressing room, and an exquisite marble spa bath with soaking tub and glass-enclosed shower. Additional bedrooms feature en-suite baths, several with private balconies, while a stylish lounge provides a comfortable retreat.
The third floor is devoted to wellness and work with a gym featuring an activity wall, steam bath and sauna, private massage spa room, additional bedrooms with en-suite baths, and beautifully appointed offices—including one connected to the primary suite by a spiral staircase and dressing room.
The lower level offers exceptional recreation with a lounge and playroom, home theater with built-in sofas, exquisite wine room, snack station, laundry room, and even a luxury dog spa, along with access to the two-car garage with lift.
With multiple covered porches, elevator access to every level, exquisite landscaping, and unparalleled waterfront amenities, Villa Cheri offers an extraordinary lifestyle of elegance, comfort, and refined coastal living.

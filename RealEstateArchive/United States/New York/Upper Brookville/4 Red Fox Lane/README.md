## Description

Set on nearly 10 private acres in the heart of Upper Brookville, Red Fox is a distinguished Gold Coast estate of exceptional character and scale. This 17-room French Tudor is marked by classic architectural details, including turrets, a slate roof, and striking 3-story bay windows that lend timeless elegance. Richly detailed interiors feature beautifully proportioned principal rooms, including a grand living room, banquet-sized dining room, inviting family room, and a serene primary suite with fireplace, sitting area, and generous mirrored closets. A spacious country eat-in kitchen and a lower entertainment level with bar and gym further enhance the home’s appeal. Outdoors, the estate opens to multi-tiered gardens, expansive terraces and patios, an in-ground pool, pool house with full kitchen, 2 full baths and guest quarters, tennis/sport court, golf hole with 3 tee boxes, apple orchard, trellis, and outdoor barbecuing area. A rare offering that captures the privacy, grace, and enduring appeal of the North Shore Gold Coast.

## Description

First Time on the Market: Grand Waterfront Estate on Two Riverfront Lots

An exceptional opportunity to own a magnificent waterfront estate—available for the first time on the market—situated on two expansive lots along the scenic Toms River. Designed for effortless entertaining and luxurious everyday living, this custom home features 5 bedrooms, 5 full bathrooms, and 3 half baths, including two conveniently located just outside the kitchen for seamless outdoor hosting without guests needing to enter the main home.

Enter through etched glass doors into a soaring foyer adorned with a grand staircase and sparkling crystal chandelier. The formal dining room is anchored by a circular bar, perfect for entertaining. A stunning great room showcases a fireplace, beamed ceilings, and a dramatic wall of windows with panoramic river views, flowing seamlessly into the breakfast room and gourmet chef's kitchen. Outfitted with cherry cabinetry, commercial-grade appliances, an oversized island with gas cooking, granite counters, and built-insincluding an icemaker and pantrythis kitchen is as functional as it is impressive.

A formal living room, also overlooking the water, completes the main level, which features rich hardwood flooring throughout. Additional highlights include a chair lift to the garage level with laundry and workshop space; and an elevator providing easy access to the second floor.

Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite boasts breathtaking river views, double closets plus a separate dressing room with cold storage, and a spacious ensuite bath. Three additional bedrooms each offer private baths and balcony access. A fourth bedroom is currently used as an office, and a bonus room with beamed ceilings serves as a versatile hobby space, home office, or studio.

Outside, enjoy multiple tiers of decks and balconies, two gazebos by the water, and a spacious dock and bulkheadingperfect for boaters and water activity enthusiasts. Adding a touch of charm and conversation-worthy character is the iconic red British telephone booth nestled on the propertyan unexpected and delightful focal point that brings whimsy to the waterfront setting. A spiral staircase, imported from the Eiffel Tower, adds a rare architectural touch, connecting the exterior to the upper level. The grounds are beautifully landscaped with vibrant perennial gardens and enhanced by a paver driveway, an attached two-car garage, a detached one-car garage with electric, and a spacious garden/storage shed.

Additional features include:
' 5-zone heating, 3-zone central air
' Two hot water heaters
' Gas heat and well-fed irrigation system

A rare and elegant offeringthis riverfront estate is truly one-of-a-kind.

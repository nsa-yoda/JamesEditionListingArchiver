DETAILED PROPERTY DESCRIPTION

Nestled on 23.44 bucolic acres in scenic Wall Township, lies one of Monmouth County's largest estates, "Mattaccino", a secure and private family compound without equal.  The gated stone entrance leads down a great oak-lined paved drive to a magnificently renovated main house flanked by your own 4-hole executive golf course on one side, and a 7-acre equestrian riding pasture on the other (suitable for vineyard).  During any season, Mattaccino is a playground for adults and children alike with an incredible array of luxurious features rarely seen together on one property.  The main house features a massive sunken living room with soaring 25 foot sky-lit ceilings and a wall of windows overlooking a resort-style backyard, a large pool cottage with massive bar, living room, sleeping nook, full kitchen, laundry, shower and 2 half baths, fire pit, tennis court, covered grill area, and huge, 3,500 square foot cobalt, ansylbrite-quartz pool with 15 stool plumbed-in swim up bar, and 20 person jetted spa.  15,000 square feet of silver travertine patio provide ample alternative seating and lounging areas while 160 Andersen windows frame magnificent views in and out.  Floor lighting highlights the marble floors, platforms, landings and stairs throughout this sun-drenched oasis.  A four foot high, 600 foot long Brazilan Cumaru fence lines the Campbell Road frontage.

​

An ipe wood-clad and sky-lit indoor pool and spa retreat is adorned with royal palms and loungers, leading to a professionally equipped gym with steam room, shower, and sauna with a Himalayan salt wall.  The home boasts 6 bars, including a cocktail and piano lounge with billiard annex and hand-tufted leather wall.  The estate features a formal dining room for 16 and view-through, two level 35-foot stone fireplace, Met-inspired old masters art gallery, barber shop, zen massage room, racquetball court, 15 seat multi-level movie theatre, concessions lobby, and full night club/video bar with karaoke stage, dance floor, wine cellar, exposed brick walls and nearby video game arcade.  The main kitchen is a gourmet’s dream with 4 ovens, 10 burners, extensive cabinetry, and double height brick-clad breakfast nook with massive birdcage chandelier.  There is a laundry center and large pantry.  The primary bedroom suite offers gorgeous views of the property, secret passages to kitchen and offices, tall ceilings, 2 massive walk-in closets, two incredible marble baths, dedicated  laundry room and a sitting area/personal office/additional adjoining accommodations for infants and small children. 

 

​For the work-at-home professional the estate features a zoom enabled situation/conference room with projection screen and a generous office and file room with extended strong wi-fi capability.  2 generators provide uninterrupted electrical service through natural gas lines while 26 zones of well-fed irrigation mean the park-like lawns stay green without cost.  There are two small ponds on the property, a fenced chicken coop with vegetable garden and 4-5 acres of peaceful, quiet undeveloped woodlands.  Connected to the main house are two 4-car epoxy-floored garages wired for electrical vehicle charging with 2-bedroom 2 bath apartments above them, separate keyed entrances, foyers, kitchens, laundry rooms, bars and outdoor second floor decks with sliders.  There is an additional one-bedroom apartment suitable for a domestic couple.  The 14-stall horse facility is over 6,500 square feet of space on 2 floors for horse lovers, or for conversion into another massive living space or party barn. 3,200 square feet of main house attic space provide limitless storage possibilities. The house features central vacuum in all living areas. 

 

Campbell Road is lined with farms, abuts Belaire Golf Course, and is minutes to the shore beaches, train station, and Garden State Parkway.  Wall Township's excellent school system and myriad commercial conveniences make Mattaccino an ideal retreat from city life, while maintaining a reasonable commuting distance to NYC and Philadelphia.  The property can accommodate a helipad, and Monmouth Executive Jet Center is a short 5-minute ride away.  The restaurants and nightlife of Asbury Park are 11 minutes away. The property is zoned R-6 and could be subdivided into 3 lots, given the 6-acre minimum.

The home was built to exacting specifications in 1987 by famed contractor William Govel, and then owned by Aston Baker, music producer Charles Huggins, noted psychiatrist R. Jiandani, a major league baseball player, and now noted attorneys Jon Biondo and Timothy Horman. 

​

Low operating costs as a result of 156 panel ground based solar array.  Enough solar energy is produced at Mattaccino to nearly eliminate all utility bills.  This efficient smart home is secured with central monitoring, motion sensors and cameras throughout, and controlled by apps for gate, HVAC, alarms, cameras, garage doors, pools, and front door locks.

## Description

A One-of-a-Kind Architectural Country Estate! Extraordinary custom sprawling ranch set on over eight private acres adjoining more than 33 acres of preserved land. Designed for grand-scale entertaining, this approximately 5,780-square-foot architectural showpiece showcases dramatic vaulted ceilings, skylights and magnificent arched laminated beams supported by custom Vermont stone bases. Multiple Pella sliding doors create a seamless connection to expansive, multi-level decking overlooking the property’s peaceful natural surroundings. The spacious single-level layout offers three bedrooms, three-and-a-half baths, an impressive great room, generous gathering spaces and a partially finished basement with an exercise room. The luxurious first-floor primary suite features a walk-in closet, jetted tub and separate shower. A wet bar, security system, central vacuum and four-car garage complete this exceptional country retreat. Conveniently located near Blair Academy, this distinctive residence offers an exceptional opportunity for those seeking a private home close to the renowned boarding school and its vibrant international community. Remarkable craftsmanship, abundant space and serene natural beauty come together in one of Blairstown’s most unique properties.

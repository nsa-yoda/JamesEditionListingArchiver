## Description

Set in the exquisite Bar B Bar Ranch, this 36-acre property boasts magnificent Grand Teton and Sleeping Indian views, live water, and wonderful privacy. The quaint homestead cabin adds historic charm to the property. A horse barn and two loafing sheds add a touch of western flair and may accommodate horses.Designed by Carney Logan Burke Architects and built by Peak Builders, this 7,587 sf custom home is a showcase of state-of-the-art finishes, fixtures, and systems. Exceptional construction and engineering are behind the remarkable walls of glass. Case optical glass from Germany and Poland is of the highest quality glass found in windows. Heated and cooled by a geothermal system, the residence complements its surroundings with its low profile, stone walls, and minimalistic design.The 1,172 sf garage has space for three vehicles. A snowmelt system in the parking area reduces the need for winter plowing. A separate 830 square foot, one-bedroom, one-bathroom guest house provides comfortable accommodations for family or guests and boasts solar energy. Discover the peaceful beauty of this exceptional Bar B Bar Ranch property. From the mournful bugle of a bull elk gathering his harem to the soft sounds of water, and from the melodious song of birds to the gentle rustling of leaves, the property invites you to experience Nature the Jackson Hole way.

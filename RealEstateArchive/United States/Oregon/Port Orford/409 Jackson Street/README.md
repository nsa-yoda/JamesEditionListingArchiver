## Description

The Point is a rare oceanfront retreat with four one-bedroom residences and sweeping views of the Pacific. Each unit has unobstructed ocean and beach views and includes upscale furnishings, high-end appliances and thoughtful touches. Features include queen beds with heated toppers, rainforest showers, heated bidet toilets, heated bathroom floors, electric fireplaces, large TVs, and folding desks for remote work. Two of the residences include full gourmet kitchens, ideal for longer stays. All furnishings convey, providing a turnkey opportunity for either continued rental use or immediate private enjoyment. Guests may gather on a private deck, perched 100 feet above the water with 270-degree panoramic views of the Pacific, to enjoy unforgettable sunsets and migrating whales. Three of the residences also feature intimate balconies off the bedrooms, perfect for a quiet morning coffee or a glass of wine at dusk. Since its inception in 2021, The Point has been operated as a highly rated short-term rental and holds Superhost status on Airbnb, drawing consistent five-star reviews and repeat visitors. Situated only 45 minutes from Bandon Dunes Golf Resort, one of the world’s premier golf destinations, this property pairs dramatic oceanfront views with access to legendary recreation. The property is also within walking distance to local cafés, dining, and outdoor outfitters, with direct access to coastal trails and the beach just steps from your door. Whether continued as a thriving vacation rental or envisioned as a private oceanfront compound, this property offers an extraordinary opportunity on the Southern Oregon Coast.

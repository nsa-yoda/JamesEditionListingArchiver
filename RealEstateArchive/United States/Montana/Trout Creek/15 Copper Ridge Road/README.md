## Description

Welcome to 15 Copper Ridge Road in beautiful Trout Creek. This impressive six bedroom, five bathroom estate offers approximately 6400 square feet of carefully designed living space surrounded by mature trees and the natural beauty of western Montana. Inspired by classic European design and built with exceptional craftsmanship, the home blends quality construction with comfort and functionality. The exterior features timeless stucco and stone finishes along with a durable composition roof designed to handle Montana weather. A spacious deck creates the perfect setting for outdoor dining, relaxing evenings, or gathering with friends while taking in the peaceful surroundings. Inside, solid mahogany floors and staircases set the tone for the level of detail carried throughout the home. Each level is constructed with true four inch concrete slabs and five zone radiant heating, providing consistent comfort year round. The kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, complete with premium appliances from Thermador and Bosch, White Cashmere granite countertops, and generous workspace. The main living area is anchored by a custom Icelandic fireplace and complemented by a tranquil indoor water feature and an additional stone fireplace with neo ceramic glass. The primary suite offers a soaking tub, tiled walk in shower, and peaceful views of the surrounding landscape. Multiple bonus rooms throughout the home provide flexible space for guests, workspace, hobbies, or storage. In addition to the main residence, the property includes a finished guest house with radiant heat and an insulated garage, as well as a fully furnished 450 square foot cabin studio with a full kitchen and bathroom. These additional spaces offer versatility for extended stays, guests, or potential rental income. Outdoor recreation is easily accessible, with convenient proximity to Schweitzer Mountain Resort in nearby Sandpoint for skiing and year round activities, as well as Lake Pend Oreille for boating, fishing, and waterfront enjoyment. From hiking and hunting to lake days and winter sports, this location offers access to a wide range of Montana and northern Idaho recreation. Whether you are seeking a full time residence, a seasonal retreat, or an investment opportunity, 15 Copper Ridge Road presents a rare chance to own a substantial property in a scenic and well connected setting.

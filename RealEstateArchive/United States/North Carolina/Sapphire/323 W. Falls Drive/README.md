## Description

Nestled within the beautiful community of Lonesome Valley and only minutes from Cashiers, this exceptional six bedroom en-suite and two half bath home—comprising a main residence and guest house—offers the perfect fusion of mountain modern elegance and comfort. On the main level, the primary en-suite is a true retreat: framed by sweeping rock-face views, it boasts a spa-like bathroom and generous his-and-her closets. A private study offers a quiet haven for reading, relaxing, or remote work. The open layout links the living room, dining area, and designer kitchen seamlessly, anchored by a stone fireplace and floor-to-ceiling windows that draw the stunning views in. Step outside to the Carolina Porch, where you’ll find an outdoor kitchen, living and dining areas, and a second stone fireplace — perfect for al fresco entertaining across seasons. Below, the terrace level hosts three guest suites, a large family room centered on a fireplace, multiple lounge and game-table zones, and a wet bar — an ideal space for gatherings, movie nights, or casual entertaining. Step outside onto the terraced lawn space with a firepit for evenings under the stars. Linked by a breezeway, the guest house offers a second primary suite and an artist studio / secondary office on the main level and on its upper floor, a spacious guest suite with its own living room and kitchenette. This layout gives flexibility for multigenerational living, creative use, or hosting extended guests. Life in Lonesome Valley is defined by natural beauty and an active, family-friendly lifestyle. Residents enjoy a pristine four-acre lake, trout pond, and over nine miles of scenic trails through meadows, streams, and waterfalls. Amenities include a state-of-the-art fitness barn, racquet sports complex, heated pool, disc golf, community gardens, and family activity spaces — all designed to bring neighbors together while embracing the wonder of the Blue Ridge Mountains. Homeowners also enjoy close proximity and priority member access to Canyon Kitchen and the Canyon Spa, two of the community’s most celebrated destinations.

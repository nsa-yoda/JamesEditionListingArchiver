## Description

Tucked away in Poland’s enchanting Masurian Lake District, Masuria Arte offers an extraordinary opportunity to own an estate that harmoniously combines luxury, natural beauty, and timeless architecture. Encompassing approximately 4,000 square meters of meticulously designed spaces and set on 110 hectares of private land, this property is a haven for those seeking serenity and exclusivity.Property Overview: • Location: Stacze near Ełk, Warmia-Masuria, Poland • Land Size: Approx. 110 hectares • Total Usable Area: Approx. 4,000 sq mLuxurious Living SpacesAt the heart of Masuria Arte lies an expansive, open-plan Living and Dining Hall, perfect for hosting grand gatherings or enjoying quiet moments with loved ones. The Grand Fireside Lounge is a cozy yet elegant space featuring plush seating and a centerpiece fireplace, inviting relaxation and conversation.The estate boasts 16 beautifully appointed guest rooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, offering the utmost comfort and privacy for family and visitors. Whether entertaining a few guests or hosting larger events, the thoughtful layout ensures every moment here is unforgettable.Unparalleled Amenities • Spa & Pool: The luxurious spa and infinity pool are seamlessly connected to the main manor house via an underground tunnel, providing easy access while maintaining privacy. Guests can unwind in the spa’s tranquil ambiance or take in the stunning lake views from the pool. • Night Lounge with Bar: Evenings come alive in the dedicated Night Lounge, a stylish space featuring a bar and a cinema corner, ideal for intimate movie nights or entertaining guests over cocktails. • Recreational Excellence: Two tennis courts, a private marina, and an underground garage with capacity for 18 cars ensure convenience and enjoyment for all residents. • Helipad: Direct access by helicopter enhances the estate’s exclusivity and ease of access.An Idyllic Lakeside SettingWith a half-kilometer shoreline along five serene, protected lakes, Masuria Arte offers breathtaking views and unparalleled access to nature. The estate’s proximity to the water is a rarity, made possible by its historical foundations, allowing for an immersive lakeside experience no longer possible under modern regulations.Effortless AccessibilityMasuria Arte is just a two-hour drive from Warsaw via the Via Baltica highway, blending convenience with complete seclusion. As you approach, scenic roads lined with linden trees and storks’ nests guide you to this remarkable retreat, where modern infrastructure meets untouched natural beauty.A Timeless LegacyMasuria Arte is not just a property; it is a destination, an experience, and a lifestyle. From its historical architecture to its state-of-the-art sustainability features, every detail has been crafted to provide a harmonious balance between tradition and modernity.Step Into a World of Elegance and TranquilityMasuria Arte invites you to discover a life of unparalleled comfort, where every day feels like an escape into nature’s most beautiful sanctuary. Whether as a private residence, a family retreat, or an exceptional venue for memorable events, this estate is a masterpiece waiting to be yours.
